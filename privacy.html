Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This policy explains what information AETHERION ("we", "us") handles when it operates the AETHERION Publisher tool and its channels on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Threads and Spotify.

Who uses the tool

AETHERION Publisher is an internal tool. Its only user is AETHERION itself, which uses it to upload and publish its own documentary videos to its own social media accounts. The tool is not available to the public, and viewers of our videos never sign in to it.

Information we handle

When we connect one of our own accounts (for example our TikTok account) to the tool, the platform gives us:

  • basic profile information for that account, such as the account ID, display name and avatar;
  • access tokens that allow the tool to upload and publish videos to that account.

We also handle the video files, titles, captions and hashtags that we create and choose to publish.

We do not collect personal information about viewers, followers or any other TikTok users through the tool. We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers on this website.

How we use it

  • to upload and publish our own videos to our own accounts;
  • to check whether an upload succeeded.

We do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone for marketing. We do not use it to build profiles of people.

Service providers

Access tokens are stored by the integration service we use to connect accounts (Composio). Video files may pass through our cloud storage (Google Drive) during upload. These providers process the data only to deliver their service to us.

Retention and deletion

We keep access tokens only while an account is connected. We can revoke access at any time in the platform's settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions), which makes the stored tokens useless. Published videos stay on the platform until we delete them there.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and does not collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a direct message to @aetherion789 on Instagram, or use the contact options on our YouTube channel.
